# SafeHaven

**NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 | Challenge: Be An Earth System Trend Detective**
**Team Binary space, Cairo, Egypt**

Live demo (team build): https://safe-haven-hazard-monitor--menatalla13.replit.app/

SafeHaven is a bilingual (English and Arabic) earthquake awareness app built on open data. It shows recorded events, never predictions, and pairs with a design concept for a safer-room capsule.

## What the live demo shows
- Place search worldwide, with a map
- Recorded earthquakes from USGS
- Open natural events from NASA EONET
- Weather from Open-Meteo
- Measured GNSS station motion (Nevada Geodetic Laboratory)
- NISAR: real GUNW products found through the NASA ASF catalog, shown as provisional quicklooks
- Preparedness drills (simulation only) and an emergency info button

## Real, provisional and concept
- **Live data:** USGS, EONET, Open-Meteo, GNSS, ASF catalog search
- **Provisional:** NISAR products are not independently validated and no displacement is published
- **Concept:** the safer-room capsule and the sensor and beacon idea are not validated by tests

## Limits
- Earthquakes cannot be predicted. Nothing here forecasts an event.
- Raw radar phase is never used for alerts.
- SafeHaven does not replace official alerts. In danger, follow local authorities.
